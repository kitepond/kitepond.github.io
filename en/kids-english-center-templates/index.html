How to choose

  • Is your learner 3–7 and play comes first? → Tinylish.
  • Are they 6–11 and love building and experiments? → Kitelish.
  • Do you teach in levels, from beginner up the ladder? → Yaylish.
  • A neighborhood club with homework help? → Peachlish.
  • A premium small-group academy with a placement visit and a Pre-A1 to B2 pathway? → Cedarlish.

All five styles ship 6 pages + a 404, an EN/VI switch, Call, Zalo and Messenger contact options (set in the config file), a trial or consultation request form and one config file, with the same Single / Agency license structure. You can't go wrong – pick by feel, and open the demos side by side.

Honest notes

  • The demos use sample content only. Teachers, tuition, timetables and addresses are made up – replace them with your real details.
